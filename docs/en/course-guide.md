This course introduces you to the technical evaluation of computer hardware and software components. You will learn to identify technical requirements, research possible hardware and software solutions, compare components, assess compatibility, and communicate justified recommendations using appropriate technical vocabulary.

The course is practical and intensive. Regular assessments let you practise the same skills you will need for the final exam and certificative project.

## Learning goals

By the end of the course, you should be able to:

- identify the technical requirements of a development or deployment scenario;
- research software and hardware components related to a technical challenge;
- compare possible solutions using appropriate technical criteria;
- evaluate compatibility, cost, performance, and suitability;
- express an informed technical opinion clearly and professionally;
- communicate technical recommendations to both specialist and non-specialist audiences.

## Evaluation structure

The final grade is divided between regular practical assessments and certificative evaluations.

| Evaluation component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Regular practical assessments | 50% |
| Certificative exam | 30% |
| Certificative project element 1: technical research component | 10% |
| Certificative project element 2: client build recommendation | 10% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

The certificative evaluations represent 50% of the final grade and evaluate achievement of competency **00SF - Évaluer des composants logiciels et matériels**.

## Double-threshold rule

Passing the course requires satisfying two separate conditions:

- earn a passing grade on each of the three certificative evaluations: the certificative exam, the technical-research project element, and the client build-recommendation project element;
- earn an overall final average sufficient to pass the course.

A sufficient overall final average therefore does not compensate for failing a certificative evaluation. Likewise, passing every certificative evaluation does not compensate for an insufficient overall final average.

## Assessment descriptions

### Sit-down test: base 2, base 16, and endianness - 5%

You will complete a short in-class test on binary, hexadecimal, and endianness. This test verifies the foundational technical vocabulary and concepts needed for later work with computer architecture and hardware.

### Component research task: What is a bus? - 7.5%

You will research the general concept of a computer bus and explain its role in allowing components of a computer system to communicate. This task focuses on the general architectural concept rather than specific bus standards.

### Recommendation task: spec a PC for a classmate - 7.5%

You will recommend a computer configuration for a classmate's personal and professional needs within a budget of $2,000. Your recommendation must identify the user's needs, justify component choices, and communicate them clearly to a non-specialist reader.

### Peer feedback task - 5%

You will review a classmate's PC recommendation and provide structured feedback. Evaluation is based on the quality and usefulness of that feedback, not on general participation.

### Plain-language task: RAID flashcards - 7.5%

You will create flashcards explaining RAID configurations, focusing on their benefits and drawbacks for a non-specialist audience. This task develops technical communication and plain-language skills.

### Ethical/technical research task: AI data centres - 7.5%

You will research AI data centres as a technical and social issue, considering hardware, energy use, cooling, infrastructure, economic impact, and possible effects on local communities. You must express an informed opinion supported by evidence.

### Evidence-based technical discussion: defend an operating system - 5%

You will contribute to an online Teams discussion defending an operating system with researched technical arguments. Evaluation is based on the relevance, evidence, clarity, and professionalism of your contribution.

### Sit-down test: PowerShell commands - 5%

You will complete a short in-class test on PowerShell commands used throughout the labs. The test focuses on practical command recognition, purpose, and basic usage.

## Certificative evaluations

### Certificative exam - 30%

The exam takes place near the end of the course. It evaluates your ability to connect the concepts, vocabulary, hardware and software components, compatibility issues, and technical decision-making processes studied during the course.

You will complete the exam individually in class. It may include both closed-notes and open-notes sections.

### Integrated final project - 20%

The final project is divided into two evaluated elements, each worth 10%. Together, these elements form one integrated project arc.

| Project element | Weight | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| Project element 1: technical research component | 10% | The student researches and explains a hardware component, peripheral, or relevant technology using appropriate technical sources. |
| Project element 2: client build recommendation | 10% | The student recommends a computer configuration or technical solution based on a client scenario, needs, constraints, compatibility, and budget. |

In this project, you must use appropriate technical vocabulary, justify your choices with evidence, and communicate your recommendation clearly to a non-specialist audience.

## Marking approach

To keep feedback fast in the intensive format, most regular practical assessments will use low-granularity marking. A typical scale may include:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Complete and clear | Meets the main expectations and shows clear understanding |
| Mostly complete | Meets most expectations with minor gaps |
| Partial | Shows some understanding but has important omissions or errors |
| Minimal or incomplete | Does not adequately address the task |
| Not submitted | No assessable submission |

Where appropriate, a short checklist may be used instead. The goal is to give you quick, useful feedback before the certificative project and exam.
