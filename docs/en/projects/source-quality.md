# Source Quality Guide

## Generally strong sources

- manufacturer documentation;
- technical specifications;
- manuals;
- official documentation;
- detailed technical reviews with clear methods.

## Sources to use carefully

- forums without verification;
- videos without sources;
- promotional pages;
- automatically generated answers;
- comparison sites without method explanations.

## Questions to ask

1. Who published the information?
2. Is the date relevant?
3. Does the source provide evidence?
4. Does the information match other reliable sources?
5. Is the source mainly trying to sell something?
